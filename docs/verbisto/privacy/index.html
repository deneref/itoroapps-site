Verbisto — Privacy Policy

Effective 17 November 2026.

Practice works offline. Your progress is kept on your iPhone and in its backup.

Data leaves your phone only if you turn on “Help improve the app” yourself: your answers and which hints you opened — under a random number, without your name, e-mail, Apple ID or dates.

It helps future versions of the trainer choose what to repeat and lets us compare versions of the trainer. Everything works the same without it. There are no ads, and the data is not shared or sold.

It is stored with Apple in iCloud until you erase what was sent or write to us. The records are linked to your iCloud account: we see its number, not your name or e-mail. Turn sending off, see what was sent or erase it in Settings.

Responsible for the data: Daniel Mokeev Toro, Spain. Questions — verbisto@itoroapps.com.

Who is responsible

Daniel Mokeev Toro, private individual, Madrid, Spain. Contact: verbisto@itoroapps.com. Users in the United Kingdom (UK App Store) are not offered sending, and no data is collected about them.

In short

Practice works offline. Your progress is kept on your iPhone and in its backup. Data leaves the device only if you switch on “Help improve the app”. You can switch it off any time in Settings.

What is sent if you agree

  • Your exercise answers: which verb form was asked, whether you answered correctly, how many seconds it took, whether it was a second try, which review interval had been scheduled, which kind of mistake the trainer recognised; if the answer wasn't fully right — what you typed (up to 30 letters; input with digits or “@” is never sent); whether you typed or tapped “I don’t know”; whether the verb is regular; the kind of practice and the version of the review schedule.
  • When during an exercise you opened a rule or the form table, and went through “Build it from parts” — which step and for which form.
  • A random install number created by the app. It contains no name, Apple ID or phone number. The records are still linked to your iCloud account.
  • The day number and the seconds counted from your first answer (not a calendar date or clock time), app and text version, interface language, experiment group — which version of the trainer you have, if we compare versions; how many records were dropped when too many piled up.

Never sent

Name, e-mail, Apple ID, phone number, address, location, calendar dates, time zone, device model, advertising identifier. We show no ads and share nothing with ad networks or data brokers.

Why

So that future versions of the trainer choose better what to review and when, recognise new kinds of mistakes, and so we can compare versions of the trainer. Without your consent everything on this phone works the same. For nothing else.

Legal basis

Your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, Arts. 6–7 LOPDGDD; for reading data on your device — Art. 5(3) Directive 2002/58/EC). You can withdraw it any time with the switch in Settings; withdrawal does not affect data already sent, but you can erase it.

If you are under 16

(under 14 in Spain, Art. 7 LOPDGDD; 13–16 elsewhere), switch sending on together with a parent.

Where it is stored

On Apple servers (iCloud, CloudKit), in the app’s public database. Apple acts as our processor on our instructions. Apple servers may be in the United States; transfers from the EU are covered by the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses. Sending requires an iCloud sign-in. We see the number of your iCloud account, not your name or e-mail. Through it, all your records are linked to that account — that is how erasing works. Apple’s server itself records when each record was created.

Retention

Until you erase what was sent yourself (Settings → “Erase sent data”) or write to us. There is no deletion by date.

Your rights

Access, copy, correction, erasure, restriction, withdrawal of consent. Fastest: Settings → “Erase sent data” deletes every record sent from this iCloud account and issues a new install number. “What is sent” shows your last record and install number. By e-mail: write to verbisto@itoroapps.com with the install number from “What is sent”; we reply within one month.

Complaints

You may complain to a data protection authority — in your country of residence or the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD, aepd.es).

Changes

We will tell you in the app before changes to this policy take effect.
