Questions

Can I practise for free first?

Yes, in two ways. Indefinido and Perfecto are free forever: no subscription, every person, full mistake breakdowns. The other tenses come with the full version. On the yearly plan the first 7 days are free, and we’ll remind you 2 days before you pay. If you decide not to carry on, cancel before those 7 days are up: iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions. The monthly plan has no free days.

How is a subscription different from Lifetime?

A subscription renews on its own until you cancel it, and you can’t share it with your family. Lifetime is a one-time purchase, and you can share it with your family through Family Sharing.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Any time: iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

What happens to my progress if my subscription ends?

All of it stays on your iPhone. The paid tenses close for practice. Indefinido and Perfecto stay open. Subscribe again and you pick up right where you left off.

Will it work if I’m learning Latin American Spanish?

Mostly. The forms in these tenses are the same there, with two differences. Vosotros isn’t used: people say ustedes instead. And for ‘today’ or ‘this week’ Latin America tends to use the indefinido, while the app teaches the usage of Spain: the perfecto.

Which tenses does it cover?

Pretérito Indefinido, Pretérito Imperfecto, Pretérito Perfecto, Pretérito Pluscuamperfecto and Futuro Simple. Indefinido and Perfecto are free forever; the rest come with the full version.

What language are the explanations in?

English. Where English speakers tend to go wrong, the explanations go through English tenses.

How do I type accents?

As soon as you type a vowel, the app shows a row of accented vowels: tap the one you need. Or do it the usual way and hold the vowel down on the iPhone keyboard.

Do I need an internet connection?

Not for practice: it works offline. You need a connection to make a purchase, and to send your answers if you’ve turned on “Help improve the app” yourself.

Which iPhone do I need?

Any iPhone running iOS 26 or later.
